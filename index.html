This Privacy Policy explains how BrainRot ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our Android application. BrainRot is developed by Sojeeb Academy. We built this app to help you reduce phone addiction and stay focused — not to collect or profit from your personal data.

In short: BrainRot does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with us or any third party. All data the app creates stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

BrainRot does not require you to create an account, and it does not send your data to our servers or to any third party. The only information the app works with is:

  • Focus session data — start/end times and duration of your focus sessions, stored only in a local database on your device.
  • App usage data — with your permission, the app reads which apps you use and for how long, so it can show you your own screen-time statistics and let you block distracting apps during a focus session. This data is read and processed entirely on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Settings and preferences — such as your chosen always-on-display quote style, blocked-app list, and timer preferences, stored locally on your device.

None of this information leaves your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

2. Permissions We Request, and Why

BrainRot requests certain sensitive Android permissions to provide its core focus features. We ask for the minimum needed to make each feature work:

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) — lets the app see which apps you've opened and for how long, so it can show you screen-time stats and enforce app blocking during focus sessions. This data is read locally and never transmitted off your device.
  • Device Admin — allows the app to lock your screen automatically when a focus session is active, to help you resist the urge to check your phone. You can revoke this at any time in Android Settings, which will also end this capability.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — used to show a blocking overlay when you try to open a distracting app during an active focus session.
  • Notifications — used to show the ongoing focus-session status and reminders.
  • Foreground Service — keeps your focus session and timer running reliably in the background while the app is active.
  • Ignore Battery Optimizations — requested so Android doesn't kill the focus timer mid-session to save battery.
  • Receive Boot Completed — lets a scheduled focus session resume correctly if your device restarts.
  • Vibrate — used for session start/end haptic feedback.

You can review or revoke any of these permissions at any time from your device's Settings app. Revoking a permission may disable the related feature but will not affect the rest of the app.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data BrainRot creates — focus session history, usage statistics it reads, and your settings — is stored locally on your device using a local database. We do not operate any backend server that receives this information, so there is no remote database of your data for us to secure, lose, or share.

4. Third-Party Services

BrainRot does not integrate any advertising network, analytics SDK, or data-sharing third-party service that collects your personal information. If this changes in a future update, we will update this policy before the change takes effect and, where required, ask for your consent.

5. Children's Privacy

BrainRot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect or transmit personal data to us at all, no such data is retained by us regardless of the user's age.

6. Your Choices

Because everything BrainRot stores lives only on your device, you are always in control:

  • Revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings > Apps > BrainRot > Permissions.
  • Clear the app's local data via Android Settings > Apps > BrainRot > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data permanently.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or for legal reasons. We will post any changes on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
