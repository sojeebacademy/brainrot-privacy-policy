This Privacy Policy applies to the BrainRotX: Stop Phone Addiction app (Android application, package: com.sojeebacademy.brainrot). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use this app. By using the app, you agree to this policy.
01

Information We Collect

BrainRot does not require an account and does not transmit your personal data to us or to any third party. Everything the app works with stays on your device:
  • Focus session data — start/end times and durations, stored locally.
  • App usage data — read locally (with your permission) to show screen-time stats and enforce app blocking during focus sessions. Never uploaded.
  • Settings and preferences — timer settings, blocked-app list, and AOD quote style, stored locally.
  • Trusted contacts — if you save trusted contacts inside the app for emergency calling, their names and phone numbers are stored locally on your device.
  • Your phone's contacts — if you grant the Contacts permission, the app reads the names and numbers in your phone's contact list on demand, only to show them on the Emergency Contacts page so you can call them during a focus session. They are not copied into the app's own storage and are never uploaded.
  • Phone call state — if you grant the Phone permission, the app checks locally whether a call is in progress, so the focus lock can step aside during a call and return afterwards. It does not read your call history, listen to or record calls, or access call content.
Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it.

No personal data leaves your device
02

How We Use Information

Since no personal information is transmitted to us, we do not use, process, sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties for any purpose — including advertising, analytics, or profiling.

Contact and phone information is used for one purpose only: letting you place a call to an emergency number, a trusted contact, or a contact from your phone while a focus session is running. When you tap the Call icon, the number is handed to your phone's own calling service to place the call; BrainRot does not keep a record of the calls you make.
03

Permissions We Request, and Why

BrainRot requests certain sensitive Android permissions to power its core focus features. Each is used only for the purpose below, and processed entirely on-device:
  • Usage Access — reads which apps you use and for how long, to show screen-time stats and enforce app blocking during a focus session.
  • Device Admin — locks your screen during an active focus session. Revocable anytime in Android Settings.
  • Display Over Other Apps — shows a blocking overlay when you try to open a distracting app mid-session.
  • Notifications — shows ongoing session status and reminders.
  • Foreground Service — keeps the focus timer running reliably in the background.
  • Ignore Battery Optimizations — prevents Android from killing the timer mid-session.
  • Receive Boot Completed — resumes a scheduled session correctly after a device restart.
  • Vibrate — haptic feedback on session start/end.
  • Phone (Make Phone Calls) — lets the Call icon on the Emergency Contacts page dial a number directly, without opening the keypad. Only numbers listed on that page (emergency numbers, your saved trusted contacts, and your phone's contacts) can be called. Standard carrier charges may apply to calls you place.
  • Read Phone State — lets the app detect whether a call is in progress, so the focus lock does not get in the way of an active call and returns to normal once the call ends. It is not used to read your phone number, call history, or call content.
  • Contacts (Read Contacts) — lets the Emergency Contacts page show your phone's contacts so you can search for and call them during a focus session. Optional: if you deny it, the page still works with emergency numbers and the contacts you saved inside the app.
The Phone, Read Phone State and Contacts permissions are requested only for the emergency calling feature. If you decline them, the rest of the app keeps working, but calling from the lock screen will not be available. You can review or revoke any of these permissions at any time from your device's Settings app.

All permissions processed on-device only

BrainRot does not integrate any advertising network, analytics SDK, or data-sharing third-party service that collects your personal information. There are no in-app purchases or payment processors in this app. If this changes in a future update, we will update this policy before the change takes effect and, where required, ask for your consent.

No ads, no analytics, no data sharing
05

Data Retention & Security

All data BrainRot creates — focus session history, usage statistics it reads, your settings, and any trusted contacts you save — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a backend server that receives this information, so there is no remote copy of your data for us to retain, lose, or share. You can clear it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data via your device settings.

Contacts read from your phone's contact list are not stored by the app; they are read when the Emergency Contacts page opens and discarded when you leave it. If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include this app's local data in your device backup, which is managed by your device and Google account under their terms, not by us.
